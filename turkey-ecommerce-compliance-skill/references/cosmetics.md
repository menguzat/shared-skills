# Cosmetics Module

## Main sources

- 5324 sayılı Kozmetik Kanunu.
- Kozmetik Ürünler Yönetmeliği, RG 08.05.2023/32184 (Mükerrer), as amended.
- `TR-COSMETIC` official sector guide.

## Classification

A product intended to contact external parts of the human body primarily for cleaning, perfuming, changing appearance, protecting, keeping in good condition or correcting body odours may fall within cosmetics law, subject to the exact statutory definition.

Do not classify a product as cosmetic if its principal represented purpose is disease treatment, disinfection/biocidal effect, or another regulated purpose.

## Visible audit

Capture:
- product function/intended use,
- INCI ingredient list where required,
- nominal content,
- warnings and directions,
- responsible-person/manufacturer information required on label/offer,
- batch/traceability information where visible,
- claims.

## Non-public compliance file

The official product-rules guide states that cosmetic safety is demonstrated through a Product Safety Report contained in the Product Information File. It also notes manufacturer responsibility and responsible technical personnel requirements.

Use `DOCUMENT_REQUIRED` for:
- Product Information File,
- Cosmetic Product Safety Report,
- notification evidence in the applicable national electronic system,
- GMP/manufacturing evidence,
- claim substantiation,
- SPF/UVA testing for sun-protection claims where relevant.

Do not say these documents do not exist solely because they are not public on the product page.

## Claims

Examples usually within cosmetic territory when substantiated:
- moisturises,
- cleanses,
- perfumes,
- improves appearance/feel.

Potential route-changing claims:
- treats eczema,
- prevents infection,
- antibacterial/disinfectant as primary function,
- cures hair loss.

## Hemp cosmetic decision

If a product contains only hemp seed oil, do not automatically classify it as a 2026 regulated hemp personal-care product. Obtain exact INCI/technical composition when needed.

If it contains cannabinoids/cannabinoid combinations meeting the 2026 regulation’s definition of a hemp-derived personal-care product, route to `hemp.md`; products within that regulation’s scope are pharmacy-only under Article 34.
