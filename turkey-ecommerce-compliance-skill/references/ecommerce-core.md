# General E-Commerce Core — Türkiye

## Main sources

- `TR-ECOM-6563`
- `TR-ECOM-LIST`

The Ministry’s current e-commerce legislation index identifies 6563 and its secondary instruments as the core legal framework for electronic commerce in Türkiye.

## Audit objectives

Determine whether the operator presents the identity and commercial information required for its role, and whether the site structure is consistent with its business model.

### Business-model questions

- Is the operator the seller, an intermediary/marketplace, or both?
- Are third-party sellers identifiable?
- Who invoices the consumer?
- Who is responsible for delivery and returns?
- Is the site using commercial electronic communications for marketing?
- Is ETBİS registration relevant to the model?

## Visible checks

Check, as applicable:
- legal/trade name,
- accessible contact information,
- address and electronic contact routes,
- MERSİS / commercial registration information where required,
- clear seller identity on marketplace product offers,
- separation between platform operator and seller where relevant,
- terms that do not obscure the contracting party,
- marketing opt-in/communication practices.

## Findings

Use `INFO_MISSING` when a required website disclosure is absent.
Use `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED` if the operator’s legal role cannot be determined from the site.
Do not conclude ETBİS non-registration solely because a public badge/link is not visible; verify the registry or operator records if necessary.
