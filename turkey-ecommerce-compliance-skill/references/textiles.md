# Textiles

## Main source

`TR-TEXTILE`: Türkiye Ürün Kuralları Veri Tabanı summary of the regulation on textile fibre names and fibre-composition labeling/marking.

## Core rule

Textile products in scope must be labeled/marked with fibre composition; the official guide states that labeling/marking must be durable, easily legible, visible and accessible, and that the producer is responsible for accuracy when the product is placed on the market.

## Website audit

For hemp/cotton/linen products:
- capture stated fibre percentages,
- identify whether “hemp” is a fibre-composition claim or merely a product name,
- compare website claim to physical-label evidence when provided,
- flag ambiguous “hemp fabric” descriptions lacking the actual composition where the consumer may reasonably be misled.

## Claims

`hemp`, `linen`, `organic cotton`, `recycled`, `natural fibre`, `handwoven` are factual/quality claims and need substantiation appropriate to the claim.

Do not treat hemp textile as a regulated hemp health product merely because the fibre comes from Cannabis sativa.
