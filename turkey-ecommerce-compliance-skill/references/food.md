# Food Module

## Main sources

- 5996 sayılı Veteriner Hizmetleri, Bitki Sağlığı, Gıda ve Yem Kanunu.
- Türk Gıda Kodeksi Gıda Etiketleme ve Tüketicileri Bilgilendirme Yönetmeliği and guides.
- `TR-FOOD-LABEL-2026`.
- `TR-FOOD-TRANSITION-2026`.
- Product-specific TGK communiqués where applicable (honey, olive oil, oils, etc.).

## General classification

Ordinary food remains food even if botanicals are used. Do not route ordinary herbal tea to medicinal-tea law solely because it contains medicinally known plants.

## Website / label data to capture

As applicable:
- legal food name,
- net quantity,
- ingredient list,
- allergen declaration,
- responsible food business operator,
- origin/provenance where required,
- storage/use instructions,
- date information,
- nutrition declaration,
- preparation instructions,
- nutrition/health claims.

The 2026 Ministry guidance states, among other updates, that herbal teas must clearly state preparation instructions on the label. The Ministry also announced a 31.12.2026 deadline for cited labeling changes in its transition explanation.

## Food-business documents

Depending on role/activity, verify the appropriate food-business registration/approval and supplier/manufacturer documents. If the site is merely reselling sealed products, do not assume the same obligations as a manufacturer without checking the exact operational model.

## Claims

Route health and nutrition claims to current TGK claim rules. Do not allow disease-treatment/prevention claims for ordinary foods.

## Hemp foods

For hemp seed, hearts, seed oil, flour or seed-derived foods:
- do not automatically apply the pharmacy-only hemp regime;
- verify exact ingredient, plant part and cannabinoid/extract status;
- request cannabinoid/THC analysis when needed to establish classification or legal thresholds;
- if cannabinoid-enriched extract/support-product positioning is present, route to `hemp.md` and potentially pharmacy-only classification.

## Product-specific subchecks

### Honey
Verify applicable honey communiqué requirements, origin/type wording and any botanical-origin substantiation. Avoid medical claims.

### Olive oil
Verify grade/category wording, origin and product-specific labeling requirements under current olive-oil rules.

### Herbal tea
Ordinary food tea: food route. If presented as a health-protective/therapeutic pharmacopoeial tea preparation, assess `MEDICINAL_HERBAL_TEA`.
