## Registry maintenance rules

1. Prefer a direct Resmî Gazete or mevzuat.gov.tr link when discoverable.
2. If a professional-body PDF includes a cover letter with a typo in the publication year but appends the official 2026 text, rely on the appended official text and record the discrepancy.
3. Record article numbers in the module file when directly verified.
4. Never infer repeal/continuation only from search-result age; verify status.
5. When an instrument has a transition deadline, record both `effective_date` and `mandatory_from` for the relevant obligation.
