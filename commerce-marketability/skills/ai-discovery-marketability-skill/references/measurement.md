# Measurement Principles

Do not use a single “AI visibility score” as the sole outcome.

Track separately:
- retrieval/discovery,
- citation,
- mention,
- prominence,
- factual absorption,
- accuracy/fidelity,
- recommendation,
- recommendation position,
- attribute association,
- competitor share,
- source path,
- referral traffic,
- conversion/business outcomes where measurable.

AI outputs are stochastic and model/platform behavior changes. Use repeated prompts, paraphrases, multiple models/surfaces, timestamps, and explicit search-on/search-off conditions where controllable.
