# Research Evidence Register

## Critical Survey of GEO (2026)
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14035
Classification: RESEARCH-SUPPORTED
Use: conceptual pipeline and evidence caution.
Key limitation: literature does not yet establish a stable, longitudinal, cross-platform causal technique for organic discoverability/downstream behavior.

## What Gets Cited: Competitive GEO in AI Answer Engines (2026)
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25517
Classification: RESEARCH-SUPPORTED, fixed-context citation setting.
Design: 252,000 controlled two-document trials across six LLMs.
Use: evidence that topical relevance and position were dominant citation-selection effects in that testbed; explicit price and recency helped; completeness/trust had smaller effects; formatting-only edits were weak.
Do NOT reinterpret as proof of organic retrieval/ranking gains.

## Incumbent Advantage: Brand Bias and Cognitive Manipulation Dynamics (2026)
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17443
Classification: RESEARCH-SUPPORTED within tested product/recommendation setup.
Use: known-brand advantage under attribute parity; small objective product advantages could displace incumbents; brand familiarity did not necessarily improve embedding retrieval in the paper's small RAG experiment.
Safety/ethics: study also tests fabricated authority claims. Never use fabricated authority; translate this only into a requirement to expose genuine evidence.

## Who Owns the AI Recommendation? (2026)
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23057
Classification: EXPLORATORY RESEARCH.
Use: supports repeated, cross-model measurement and category ownership/displacement concepts.
Do not generalize sampled category/model statistics as universal behavior.
