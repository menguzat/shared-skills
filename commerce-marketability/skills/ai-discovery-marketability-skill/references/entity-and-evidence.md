# Entity and Evidence Architecture

## Entity clarity
Establish canonical names, URLs, identifiers, relationships, locations, people/authors, product groups/variants, services, and policies.

## Proposition ownership
A proposition is a specific statement the business wants humans and machines to be able to establish, e.g.:
- product uses material X,
- service is available in geography Y,
- product supports measurement range Z,
- repair policy lasts N years.

Each proposition should map to evidence. The strongest evidence is real-world truth made explicit, current, inspectable, and—where appropriate—corroborated independently.

Never create fake corroboration, fake reviews, fake experts, fake certifications, or synthetic reputation signals.
