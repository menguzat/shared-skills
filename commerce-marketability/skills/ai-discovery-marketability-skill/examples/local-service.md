# Example: Local Service

Important machine-visible facts:
- canonical business name,
- exact service categories,
- service area,
- opening/booking hours,
- address/phone where public,
- pricing model or ranges when possible,
- booking method,
- qualifications/licenses where relevant and verifiable,
- genuine reviews,
- clear LocalBusiness structured data,
- consistent business profiles.

Recommendation advantage often comes from constraint fit: location, appointment timing, language, specialization, accessibility, pricing, or verified expertise.
