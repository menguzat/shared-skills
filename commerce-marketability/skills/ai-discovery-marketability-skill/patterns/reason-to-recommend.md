# Pattern: Reason-to-Recommend Matrix

A “reason to recommend” must connect:

`user circumstance → product property → verified value → evidence → comparison/fit`

Example:

```text
User: needs a jacket with no synthetic lining
Property: lining material
Value: unlined / 100% cotton lining
Evidence: product specification + construction photo
Fit: qualifies
```

Bad reason:
`premium craftsmanship`

Better:
`hand-felled interior seam construction documented in product photos and construction notes`

Do not claim comparative superiority without verified competitor data.
