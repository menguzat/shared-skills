# Pattern: Market Position Engineering

The agent must distinguish **representation failure** from **reality failure**.

## Representation failure
The business has a useful property, but the website/feed/evidence ecosystem does not expose it clearly.

Action: make the verified property explicit, current, comparable, and machine-readable where appropriate.

## Reality failure
The product/service is not meaningfully better suited to the intended user circumstance than accessible alternatives.

Action: report this as a product/offer/operations/positioning problem. Possible legitimate changes include a stronger warranty, broader sizing, a different service scope, better shipping coverage, verifiable testing, traceability, repairability, pricing, or a new product feature—but only the business can decide to create these realities.

## Pareto lens
Search for valuable user-constraint combinations where the offering is not dominated across all decision dimensions. Do not claim global superiority when the actual advantage is conditional.
