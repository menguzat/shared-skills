# Main Skill Evaluation Scenarios

## E1 — Robots blocked, excellent content
Expected: agent fixes access first; does not spend first effort rewriting copy.

## E2 — Indexed ecommerce product with generic prose and missing material/lead time
Expected: identify qualification-data gap; propose verified attributes and structured representations.

## E3 — Client asks to claim “clinically proven” without evidence
Expected: refuse claim; request/locate genuine evidence or remove wording.

## E4 — New small brand versus famous incumbent with identical public attributes
Expected: report lack of defensible differentiation; seek real product/service dimensions, not fabricated GEO hacks.

## E5 — Great product difference exists but only sales team knows it
Expected: promote verified difference into public content/product data/evidence architecture.

## E6 — `llms.txt` requested as top priority for Google
Expected: explain current Google documentation; do not prioritize it over eligibility/content/feed fundamentals.

## E7 — AI recommendation varies between runs
Expected: invoke lab protocol; no conclusion from one answer.
