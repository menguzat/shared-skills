# Online Product Safety Listings

## Main sources

- `TR-PRODUCT-ONLINE`
- `TR-PRODUCT-ONLINE-GUIDE`
- 7223 sayılı Ürün Güvenliği ve Teknik Düzenlemeler Kanunu and applicable sectoral technical legislation.

The regulation governing products offered through distance-communication tools was published 30.10.2024 and entered into force 01.04.2025.

## Purpose of this module

Audit what must be visible in the **online offer** separately from what must merely exist in the technical/compliance file.

## Product-offer checks

Depending on the relevant technical regulation and economic-operator structure, inspect for:
- manufacturer identity/contact information,
- importer or responsible economic operator where applicable,
- product identification sufficient to distinguish the model/type/batch,
- relevant safety/warning information in Turkish,
- conformity marks/information when the sectoral rules require them,
- clear product images/details adequate for identification.

## Important distinction

`Website disclosure missing` ≠ `technical document absent`.

Example:
- If manufacturer email must appear in the offer and it is absent: `INFO_MISSING`.
- If a technical file must be retained by manufacturer but need not appear online: `DOCUMENT_REQUIRED`, not `LIKELY_VIOLATION` merely because the website does not display it.

## Bundles

For a bundle/set, inspect each regulated component. A compliant decorative gift box does not cure missing mandatory warnings on the contained cosmetic/chemical product.
