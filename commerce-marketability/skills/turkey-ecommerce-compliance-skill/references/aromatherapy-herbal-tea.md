# Aromatherapeutic Products and Medicinal Herbal Teas — 2 July 2026

## Sources

- `TR-AROMA-2026`
- `TR-MED-TEA-2026`
- RG 02.07.2026/33298.

## Aromatherapeutic products

The 2026 regulation concerns aromatherapeutic products presented for health-protective, therapeutic, treatment-supporting or maintenance-of-wellbeing effects and covers the regulated standardized essential/fixed oils and products defined by that instrument.

### Auditor rule
Do **not** classify every essential oil as aromatherapeutic.

First determine intended use and legal presentation:
- therapeutic / health / wellbeing use -> assess the aromatherapeutic regulation;
- cosmetic use -> cosmetics;
- food flavour use -> food/aroma rules;
- room-fragrance use -> room-fragrance/chemical/general-product rules.

If the product is genuinely within the aromatherapeutic regulatory regime, apply the sale/licensing channel rules from the current regulation; do not propose removing the word “aromatherapy” as a way to disguise the same therapeutic intended use.

## Medicinal herbal teas

The 2026 medicinal-herbal-tea regulation covers medicinal herbal tea preparations described as having health-protective/therapeutic effects and, according to the official reproduced text, covers industrially or magistrally prepared medicinal herbal teas that are sold only in pharmacies.

### Auditor rule
Ordinary food herbal tea is not medicinal herbal tea merely because it contains herbs.

Route ordinary tea to `food.md` unless evidence shows it is presented/authorized as a medicinal herbal tea under the 2026 regime.

## Trigger examples

### Ordinary-food leaning
- “lemongrass fruit tea”
- ingredients + flavour profile + brewing directions
- no disease/health-treatment positioning

### Medicinal/aromatherapeutic leaning
- “for treatment of …”
- “supports treatment”
- pharmacopoeial/monograph standardization plus therapeutic use
- dosing/posology framed for a medical effect

## Gray-zone action

If product copy is neutral but supplier documentation says “aromatherapeutic product” or “medicinal herbal tea”, the legal status follows the product’s actual regulatory identity, not merely the website copy. Request the licence/registration/classification documentation.
