# Detergents and Biocides

## Detergents

Source: `TR-DETERGENT`.

The official product-rules guide describes requirements including surfactant biodegradability, restrictions, additional label information including fragrance allergens, manufacturer-held information for authorities/health personnel, and certain phosphorus restrictions.

### Routing
A soap/product intended mainly for laundry or surface cleaning should be assessed under cleaning/detergent rules rather than cosmetics.

Use `DOCUMENT_REQUIRED` for required technical/test information not expected to be public.

## Biocides

Source: `TR-BIOCIDE`.

The official guide states that biocidal products are subject to regulatory procedures including inventory registration, registration and/or authorization depending on product type and applicable rules.

### Trigger claims/functions
- disinfects,
- kills bacteria/viruses/fungi as a primary function,
- mosquito/insect repellent,
- pest control,
- antimicrobial surface treatment.

Do not assume a “natural” formulation is outside biocidal law.

### Soap examples

- “olive-oil hand/body soap” -> likely cosmetic route.
- “laundry olive-oil soap” -> detergent/cleaning route.
- “natural mosquito-repellent body spray” -> biocide review trigger, even if made only from essential oils.
- “kills 99.9% of bacteria” -> biocide trigger unless another specific legal route clearly applies.

If authorization status cannot be verified from public information, request the product’s biocidal registration/authorization evidence rather than declaring it absent.
