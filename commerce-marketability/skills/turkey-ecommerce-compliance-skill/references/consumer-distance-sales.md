# Consumer Law and Distance Sales

## Main sources

- 6502 sayılı Tüketicinin Korunması Hakkında Kanun.
- `TR-DISTANCE`: Mesafeli Sözleşmeler Yönetmeliği, RG 27.11.2014/29188, as amended.

## Core audit areas

Before the consumer is bound, inspect whether the site provides the required pre-contract information applicable to the transaction, including as relevant:
- essential characteristics of the goods/services,
- seller/provider identity and contact information,
- total price including taxes and foreseeable additional charges,
- payment, delivery and performance arrangements,
- complaint mechanisms,
- withdrawal/cancellation right and method,
- circumstances where the withdrawal right does not apply,
- return/refund mechanics.

## Checkout behavior

Inspect:
- whether the final order action clearly indicates a payment obligation;
- whether optional paid items are preselected;
- whether delivery cost appears before purchase;
- whether contract/pre-information text is accessible and durable-record practices are reasonable;
- whether refund and return statements contradict mandatory consumer rights.

## Withdrawal-right exceptions

Do not assume every food, cosmetic or hygiene product is automatically excluded from withdrawal. Apply the specific statutory/regulatory exception and the factual conditions. Where packaging/seal opening matters, record that condition rather than issuing a blanket exclusion.

## Common findings

- `INFO_MISSING`: total price or delivery charge appears only after the consumer has effectively committed.
- `LIKELY_VIOLATION`: site states “no returns under any circumstances” for ordinary distance-sale goods without a valid exception.
- `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED`: bespoke/made-to-order product where personalization level must be assessed against the applicable withdrawal exception.
