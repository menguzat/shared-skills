# Source and Evidence Policy

Priority order:
1. current official platform/standards documentation,
2. primary peer-reviewed or clearly described empirical research,
3. reproducible preprints with methods/results available,
4. high-quality industry research,
5. practitioner observations as hypotheses only.

For every external rule record: source, retrieval date, platform/model/version if known, scope, and whether it is normative or observational.

Never cite a study beyond its design. In particular, fixed-context RAG citation experiments do not demonstrate organic crawling, retrieval, or durable business outcomes.
