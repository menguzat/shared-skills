# Structured Commerce and Agentic Readiness

## Structured commerce
For applicable ecommerce sites, audit both page markup and external catalog/feed systems. Product information is operational marketing data.

High-value fields include:
- stable product ID/SKU/GTIN/MPN where applicable,
- title/description,
- canonical URL,
- images,
- price/currency,
- availability,
- brand,
- category,
- variants,
- material/color/size,
- shipping,
- returns,
- lead time where supported,
- seller context.

## Agent-friendly websites
Google web.dev guidance notes that agents may interpret screenshots, DOM, and accessibility-tree semantics. Build interfaces with semantic controls, accessible names, explicit states, stable navigation, and deterministic actions.
