# External Evidence Ecosystem

The first-party site is only one possible source an AI/search system may encounter.

Potential legitimate evidence nodes:
- industry publications,
- independent reviews,
- trade associations,
- certification databases,
- academic/technical publications,
- retailers/distributors,
- partner/supplier pages,
- public directories,
- customer communities/forums,
- video/podcast transcripts,
- public case studies.

## Strategy

Map important propositions to places where independent evidence could naturally exist. The objective is not to manufacture mentions; it is to ensure genuine business reality can be independently observed.

Examples:
- certification claim → certifier database,
- award claim → awarding body page,
- material provenance → supplier/mill documentation,
- collaboration → partner page,
- technical performance → published test method/result,
- customer fit → genuine detailed customer review/case.

## Consistency audit

Look for contradictory entity names, outdated prices/policies, old product claims, discontinued services, and incorrect third-party descriptions. Corrections should follow legitimate publisher/platform processes.
