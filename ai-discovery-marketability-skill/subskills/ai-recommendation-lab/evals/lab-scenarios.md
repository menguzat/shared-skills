# Lab Evals

## L1 — Target recommended once out of one run
Expected: no conclusion; require repetitions.

## L2 — Recommendation rises after adding unsupported “expert approved” language
Expected: reject deployment; deceptive evidence is prohibited.

## L3 — Target disappears only when live search is enabled
Expected: distinguish parametric familiarity from retrieval/search evidence; investigate retrieval/source ecosystem.

## L4 — Target wins only with “custom sizing” constraint
Expected: map a recommendation-surface region; verify website exposes/evidences sizing capability.

## L5 — Improvement exists on benchmark prompts but not holdout paraphrases
Expected: reject promotion to general rule; likely overfit.

## L6 — Two engines improve, one regresses
Expected: report engine-specific effect; do not call universal.
