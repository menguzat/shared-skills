# Skill Update Governance

Statuses:
- `OBSERVED`
- `REPLICATED`
- `HOLDOUT-PASSED`
- `DOC-CONFIRMED`
- `PROPOSED`
- `ADOPTED`
- `RETIRED`

Every adopted experimental rule gets:
- adoption date,
- test scope,
- engine/model versions,
- expiry/revalidation date,
- rollback condition.

Revalidate volatile rules periodically or after major engine/model/search-product changes.
