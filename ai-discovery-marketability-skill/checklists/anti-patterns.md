# Anti-Patterns

Reject or deprioritize:
- `llms.txt` as a substitute for technical SEO or content quality,
- fabricated citations/experts/reviews/certifications,
- mass-produced location/keyword pages without unique value,
- one FAQ page per prompt phrasing,
- hidden AI-only text differing materially from user-visible content,
- schema containing claims absent from visible pages,
- stale product feed prices/stock,
- generic “best/premium/sustainable” claims without defined evidence,
- rewriting content purely to exploit a single experimental model quirk,
- measuring success from one model answer,
- equating training-crawler access with search/recommendation inclusion,
- treating citation probability inside a supplied context as proof of organic retrieval,
- sacrificing usability/accessibility/performance for speculative AI optimization.
