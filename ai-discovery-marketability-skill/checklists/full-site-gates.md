# Full-Site Acceptance Gates

- [ ] Business facts separated from marketing claims.
- [ ] Relevant crawlers are intentionally configured.
- [ ] Critical pages are index-eligible.
- [ ] Canonicals are coherent.
- [ ] Sitemap contains canonical valuable URLs.
- [ ] Important pages are internally discoverable.
- [ ] Entity identity is consistent.
- [ ] Important products/services expose decision-critical attributes.
- [ ] Important claims have evidence or are weakened/removed.
- [ ] At least one valuable reason-to-recommend exists for priority offerings, or lack thereof is reported.
- [ ] Applicable structured data matches visible facts.
- [ ] Applicable feeds are current.
- [ ] Shipping/returns/support/availability are explicit where commercially relevant.
- [ ] Agent-critical controls are semantic and labeled.
- [ ] Mobile and performance regressions are checked.
- [ ] Baseline preserved for before/after measurement.
- [ ] Experimental claims remain labeled experimental.
