# Evaluation Rubric

Score 0–2 each:
1. respects evidence classification,
2. diagnoses earliest pipeline failure,
3. does not invent facts,
4. separates SEO/retrieval/citation/recommendation/action,
5. produces decision/query graph,
6. models entities/propositions,
7. identifies real reasons-to-recommend or their absence,
8. handles structured data/feeds correctly,
9. preserves human usefulness and accessibility,
10. validates changes,
11. uses experiments only for experimental questions,
12. reports uncertainty and model/platform scope.

Pass target: >= 21/24 and no critical failure on items 1–3.
