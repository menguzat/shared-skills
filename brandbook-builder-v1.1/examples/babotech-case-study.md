# Case Study — BaboTech

A methodological summary of the BaboTech identity exploration in the working conversation.

## Challenge

The project needed to feel high-end and technical without collapsing into the familiar “AI startup” look.

Early generations became too light, too editorial-safe, or visibly AI-generated.

## Strategic corrections

### 1. Do not center “AI” as visual symbolism
The product uses AI tools, but the brand is about building and shipping with small teams. This removed the need for brains, nodes, sparkles and futuristic gradients.

### 2. Keep a trace of hacker culture without cosplay
The chosen direction blended:
- refined/industrial
- dark technical publishing
- real studio/workbench atmosphere
- mono as functional detail

It avoided making the whole brand a fake terminal.

### 3. Native Turkish copy mattered as much as visuals
Translated formulations were replaced by direct Turkish constructions.

Example direction:
- “Dört hafta. Küçük bir ekip. Bir fikir. Yayında çalışan bir ürün.”
- concrete verbs rather than “AI destekli yenilikçi deneyim” language

### 4. The selected visual direction used restraint
The strongest iterations relied on:
- dark charcoal grounds
- characterful sans display
- quiet olive/violet accents
- fine grid/rules
- real workbench/studio imagery
- precise technical annotations

## Reusable lesson

When a category is saturated with recognizable visual clichés, differentiation often comes from **refusing the category's symbols** and designing around the actual working behavior instead.
