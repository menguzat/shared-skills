# External References & Further Study

These are references for method, not visual styles to copy.

## Public design / agent-skill references

### Avoid AI Design — ungspirit / funboy322
https://github.com/funboy322/avoid-ai-design/blob/main/SKILL.md

Useful for:
- identifying generic AI-generated frontend/design tells
- separating code-level and pixel-level evidence
- P0/P1/P2 triage
- “one committed direction” principle
- avoiding second-order defaults (the same tasteful redesign every time)

### Aesthetic Directions — Avoid AI Design reference
https://github.com/funboy322/avoid-ai-design/blob/main/references/aesthetic-directions.md

Useful for:
- naming concrete aesthetic directions
- committing to type + palette + layout + motion + signature detail
- distinguishing brutalist, editorial, Swiss, industrial, refined, playful, organic, etc.

### Kai Taste — Design Taste Engineering Framework
https://github.com/majiayu000/claude-skill-registry/blob/main/skills/design/kai-taste/SKILL.md

Useful for:
- treating taste as a control system
- visual cohesion
- interaction density
- diagnosing over-polish / trust distortion
- critic/refinement passes

### Minimalist Taste — amplifier-bundle-taste
https://github.com/michaeljabbour/amplifier-bundle-taste/blob/main/skills/minimalist-taste.md

Useful selectively for:
- explicit negative constraints
- typography hierarchy
- spacing discipline
- subtle motion

Do not turn its specific warm-minimal aesthetic into a universal default.

## Public brand/content references

### Mailchimp Content Style Guide
https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/
https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/voice-and-tone/
https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/writing-principles/

Study for:
- operational distinction between voice and tone
- clear/useful/appropriate writing principles
- active voice and concrete language
- writing rules that teams can actually apply

Do not copy Mailchimp's personality or humor.

### NASA Brand Guidelines
https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/brand-guidelines/

Study for:
- disciplined mark usage
- protected identity elements
- supporting graphic standards
- institutional consistency across many applications

Do not reuse NASA identifiers or assets.

## Digital design-system references

### U.S. Web Design System — Design Tokens
https://designsystem.digital.gov/design-tokens/
https://designsystem.digital.gov/design-tokens/color/theme-tokens/

Study for:
- role-based tokens
- discrete spacing/type/color decisions
- designer/developer communication
- separating semantic roles from raw values

### W3C Web Accessibility Initiative / WCAG
https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/

Use when brand systems extend into digital products. Brand colors and typography do not override accessibility requirements.

## Case-study lesson carried into this skill

The supplied Old Skool identity brief treats identity as a “visual operating system,” explicitly prioritizing the core brand idea, typography and photography/movement before logo ornament. It also separates a historical memory layer from the brand’s present-day behavior, a useful model for any brand with real heritage.
