# Fashion Closed-Loop Choreography Guide

E-commerce motion video must feel calm, luxurious, and non-distracting. If a model walks across the screen, spins, or turns around, it breaks usability on product cards and creates jarring jump cuts when looping.

---

### The 4 Phases of Closed-Loop Micro-Motion

```
Phase 1: Initial Anchor (0.0s - 1.0s)
  └─ Model stands poised, centered, looking confident into 35mm lens.

Phase 2: Inhale & Micro-Sway (1.0s - 3.5s)
  └─ Gentle chest inhale, subtle 2-degree shoulder or weight shift.
  └─ Fabric catches lighting highlights; drape and pleats expand slightly.

Phase 3: Exhale & Harmonic Return (3.5s - 5.5s)
  └─ Gentle exhale, weight settles smoothly back to center.
  └─ Fabric folds return naturally to neutral hanging state.

Phase 4: Seamless Match (5.5s - 6.0s)
  └─ Exact posture and silhouette match to Phase 1.
```

---

### Prompting Rules for Seamless Loops

1. **Always Specify the Exact Return:**
   - ✅ *"The model stands poised, gently inhales and performs a very subtle micro-sway, allowing the fabric to ripple softly, then smoothly and effortlessly returns to the exact starting standing posture."*
   - ❌ *"The model poses and moves."*

2. **Fix the Camera:**
   - ✅ *"Stationary camera, locked on tripod, 35mm prime lens, clean studio background."*
   - ❌ *"Dynamic camera swooping around the model."*

3. **Avoid Hand/Finger Motion:**
   - Keeping hands resting relaxed at the sides or in pockets prevents the AI from generating extra or distorted fingers during movement.

4. **Duration Rule:**
   - 6 seconds is the optimal duration for subtle micro-motion loops without model drift.
   - 8 seconds is recommended when multiple reference images / accessories are attached.
