# Fabric Physics & Tactile Motion Lexicon

When directing AI video models (Veo 3.1, Kling AI, etc.) for fashion e-commerce, generic terms like *"the clothes move"* produce low-quality, unnatural animations. Use precise physical and textural descriptors mapped to each material category:

---

### 1. Cupro / Rayon / Silk Blends
- **Visual Identifiers:** Liquid sheen, high drape coefficient, soft light reflections, micro-ripples.
- **Motion Descriptors:**
  - *"fluid cascading drape"*
  - *"subtle liquid-like ripples responding to breath"*
  - *"silk-touch sheen catching directional ambient light"*
  - *"weightless hemline floating gracefully"*
- **Avoid:** Stiff folds, cardboard creases, sharp snapping.

---

### 2. Raw Hemp / Heavy Linen / Canvas
- **Visual Identifiers:** Visible organic slubs, matte dry texture, structured volume, earthy handloom grain.
- **Motion Descriptors:**
  - *"substantial architectural volume with tactile raw texture"*
  - *"gentle, grounded shift showing the structured sleeve drape"*
  - *"matte organic handloom weave catching soft studio lighting"*
  - *"heavy, deliberate fabric fall without synthetic bounce"*
- **Avoid:** Ultra-glossy highlights, liquid fluttering, excessive stretching.

---

### 3. Tencel / Lyocell / Pleated Micro-Fibers
- **Visual Identifiers:** Sharp parallel creases, architectural vertical lines, crisp accordion expansion.
- **Motion Descriptors:**
  - *"crisp elasticity of the sharp vertical micro-pleats"*
  - *"accordion pleat expansion and contraction during gentle weight shift"*
  - *"heavy fluid movement with sharp dimensional shape retention"*
- **Avoid:** Flattening or smoothing out of pleats, irregular wavy wrinkles.

---

### 4. Embroidered, Beaded & Embellished Surfaces
- **Visual Identifiers:** Raised stitch relief, organic trail beads, metallic thread, light scatter.
- **Motion Descriptors:**
  - *"raised hand-stitched embroidery catching glints of warm studio light"*
  - *"delicate beadwork reflecting subtle directional highlights during posture micro-adjustment"*
  - *"tactile textural relief along the jacket seams and placket"*
- **Avoid:** Morphing beads, floating embroidery detached from fabric.

---

### 5. Modular Cords, Ties & Lacing Systems
- **Visual Identifiers:** Saturated braided cords, metallic eyelets, hanging ends, knotted pankou closures.
- **Motion Descriptors:**
  - *"interchangeable braided cords oscillating gently with model breath"*
  - *"hanging ties swaying subtly against the tiered skirt silhouette"*
  - *"tensioned lacing holding structure through eyelet loops"*
- **Avoid:** Tangling cords, ropes phasing through garment panels.
