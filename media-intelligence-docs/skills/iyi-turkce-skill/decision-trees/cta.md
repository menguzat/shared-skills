# Decision tree: CTA

1. What happens after tap/click?
2. Is that outcome obvious from context?
3. Can the verb name it directly?
4. Is there risk/cost/commitment?
   - make consequence more explicit.
5. Is CTA primary or secondary?
6. Does `Devam` hide a meaningful transition?
   - replace with specific outcome.
7. Does brand voice make label less predictable?
   - clarity wins.
