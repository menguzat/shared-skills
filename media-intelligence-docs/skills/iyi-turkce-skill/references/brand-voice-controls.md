# Brand voice controls

## Voice vs. tone

Voice = stable personality.
Tone = situation-specific adjustment.

## Voice dimensions

Define:
- directness;
- formality;
- warmth;
- technical density;
- boldness;
- playfulness;
- metaphor;
- evidence density.

## Guardrails

A voice profile must include:
- what it is;
- what it is not;
- preferred sentence behaviors;
- forbidden failure modes;
- situation overrides.

## Situation override hierarchy

Safety/legal/error/checkout clarity
> brand voice.

Technical specification
> metaphor.

Evidence uncertainty
> confidence styling.

## Consistency

Do not force identical rhythm into every channel.

A brand may sound:
- conceptual on homepage;
- exact on spec sheet;
- calm in support;
- terse in UI;
while retaining shared lexical preferences.
