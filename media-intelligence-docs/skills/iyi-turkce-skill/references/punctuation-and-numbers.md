# Punctuation and numbers

## Authority

Use current TDK for uncertain general punctuation and spelling.

## Principle

Punctuation exposes grammar and information structure.

It must not be used as a substitute for fixing a broken sentence.

## Comma

Check:
- list separation;
- parenthetical/inserted phrases;
- ambiguity;
- clause boundaries according to current Turkish conventions.

Do not place commas by breathing rhythm alone.

## Colon

Useful when the first clause explicitly prepares:
- explanation;
- list;
- result;
- quoted/formulated material.

## Semicolon

Use sparingly in digital copy.
Prefer sentence split when relation remains clear.

## Dash

Brand/editorial copy may use dash for controlled emphasis.
Do not import English em-dash frequency mechanically into Turkish.

## Numbers

For measured/specification-heavy content:
- use digits where scanning matters;
- keep units consistent;
- preserve decimal/thousands conventions for target locale;
- do not change numeric precision during editing.

## Percent vs. percentage point

Do not call a move from 20% to 25% a "5% increase" when you mean "5 percentage points". Check actual relative increase separately.

## Dates

Choose one project format and use it consistently.
Never silently swap day/month order in translation.
