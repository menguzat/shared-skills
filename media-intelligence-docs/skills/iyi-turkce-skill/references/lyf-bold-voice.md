# LYF-BOLD voice profile

This is an original voice abstraction inspired by LYF.lab's public communications.

## Voice axis

| Axis | Position |
|---|---|
| timid ↔ assertive | assertive |
| decorative ↔ material | material |
| generic ↔ specific | specific |
| product-only ↔ systems | systems |
| corporate ↔ editorial | editorial-technical |
| playful ↔ serious | serious |
| verbose ↔ compressed | compressed |
| hype ↔ evidence | evidence |

## Core move

**Reframe a category, then ground it.**

Example:
`Tarım tedarik değildir. Kumaşın ilk tasarım kararıdır.`

Ground:
`Ekim sıklığı, hasat zamanı ve lif ayırma yöntemi; lif uzunluğunu ve sonraki işlem seçeneklerini değiştirir.`

## Contrast

Use contrast to reveal a distinction, not to produce drama.

Good:
`Geri dönüşüm atığı yönetir. Malzeme seçimi, atığın doğup doğmayacağını belirler.`

Risky:
`Eski dünya çöpe inanır. Biz geleceğe.`

The second is dramatic but semantically thin.

## Evidence cadence

After one or two conceptual lines, provide:
- process;
- number;
- material behavior;
- origin;
- test;
- boundary;
- tradeoff.

## Avoid generic sustainability language

Words such as `sürdürülebilir`, `döngüsel`, `rejeneratif`, `doğal`, and `izlenebilir` should name a concrete mechanism.

Ask:
- What closes the loop?
- What is regenerated?
- Which input is natural?
- How is traceability maintained?
- Where can the chain break?

## Creative devices

Allowed:
- antithesis;
- reversal;
- parallelism;
- controlled repetition;
- rhetorical question;
- material personification, sparingly;
- temporal scale;
- system boundary.

## Failure modes

### Manifesto soup
Many big concepts, no evidence.

### Fragment addiction
Every sentence has 3–6 words. Rhythm becomes mechanical.

### Eco-poetry
Nature metaphors replace mechanism.

### False certainty
Strong tone upgrades uncertain evidence.

### Brand ego
Copy talks about the company's courage more than the material/user problem.

## Recommended page rhythm

Hero:
- 1 sharp thesis;
- 1 category/explanation line;
- 1 CTA.

Section:
- thesis;
- proof/detail;
- implication.

Technical section:
- plain heading;
- concrete facts;
- minimal voice styling.

Footer/legal:
- neutral.
