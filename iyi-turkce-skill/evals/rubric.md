# Evaluation rubric

Score each dimension 0–4.

## A. Semantic fidelity
4 — no fact, modality, quantity, actor, or causal drift.
0 — meaning materially changed.

## B. Natural Turkish
4 — reads as native contemporary Turkish.
0 — word-for-word / syntactically broken.

## C. Economy
4 — no meaningful redundancy; concise without information loss.
0 — heavily bloated or over-compressed.

## D. Terminology
4 — consistent and audience-appropriate.
0 — inconsistent/invented/misleading.

## E. Orthography
4 — current standard observed.
0 — repeated spelling/punctuation failures.

## F. Digital fitness
4 — hierarchy, scan, CTA, component fit excellent.
0 — unusable in destination.

## G. Voice
4 — context-appropriate and consistent.
0 — voice overwhelms meaning or mismatches situation.

## H. Evidence discipline
4 — claims match evidence and uncertainty.
0 — invented or inflated claims.

### Hard fail
Any of:
- fabricated fact;
- stronger guarantee than source;
- changed negation;
- destructive UI ambiguity;
- code/identifier corruption;
- illegal/unverified claim introduced by stylistic rewrite.
