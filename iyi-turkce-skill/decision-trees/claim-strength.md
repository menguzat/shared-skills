# Decision tree: claim strength

1. Is claim factual?
   - require direct evidence.

2. Comparative?
   - define comparator and dimension.

3. Superlative?
   - define universe and evidence; otherwise weaken.

4. Environmental/health/performance?
   - require stronger evidence and compliance check.

5. Is evidence pilot/preliminary?
   - retain qualifier.

6. Is statement aspiration?
   - mark as goal/aim, not result.

7. Does bold voice pressure you to delete uncertainty?
   - do not delete it.
