# English interference: high-value watchlist

The purpose is not to ban English influence. It is to stop accidental transfer that makes Turkish less natural or changes meaning.

## Article shadow

English:
`a system`, `a leader`, `a solution`

Do not automatically create:
`bir sistem`, `bir lider`, `bir çözüm`

Test whether Turkish needs `bir`.

## Explicit pronoun shadow

English requires overt subjects/possessives more often.

Watch:
- `onun performansı`;
- `onların verileri`;
- repeated `o`.

If suffix/context already carries the referent, remove unless contrast is intended.

## `do` → `yapmak`

English:
`He sent it. I did too.`

Natural Turkish may be:
`O gönderdi. Ben de gönderdim.`

Not automatically:
`Ben de yaptım.`

## `or` → `ya da`

Translate the logical/question structure.

`True or false?`
may naturally be:
`Doğru mu, yanlış mı?`

## `as` / adverb → `olarak`

Watch chains such as:
`stratejik olarak`, `aktif olarak`, `spesifik olarak`

Some are legitimate. Others are analytic shadows that can become:
`stratejik kullanmak`, `etkin kullanmak`, `özellikle`, or a different sentence.

## `make sure`

Do not give one permanent Turkish translation.

Depending on function:
- verify → `kontrol edin`;
- ensure condition → `... olduğundan emin olun`;
- instruction → direct imperative;
- prevention → `... yapmayın / ... sağlayın`.

## `quite`

Intensity depends on context. Do not map every instance to `oldukça`.

Possible Turkish outputs:
- hayli;
- epey;
- oldukça;
- çok;
- bütünüyle different restructuring.

## `early/late`

Historical/period labels may need:
- ilk dönem;
- son dönem;
- erken/geç (if domain-established).

Use domain convention.

## English noun pile

Do not convert five English nouns into five Turkish genitives.

First determine:
- ownership;
- purpose;
- object;
- method;
- type;
- source;
- audience.

Then build Turkish.

## `of`

English `of` relations are not all genitives.

Translate relation:
- source;
- part;
- content;
- topic;
- material;
- measurement;
- ownership.

## `for`

Can express:
- purpose;
- beneficiary;
- duration;
- exchange;
- audience;
- reason;
- suitability.

Do not automatically use `için`.

## `with`

Can express:
- instrument;
- accompaniment;
- possession;
- manner;
- cause;
- feature.

Do not automatically use `ile`.

## Marketing calques

Flag phrases that sound like translated global-brand boilerplate:
- `fark yaratmak`;
- `deneyimi bir üst seviyeye taşımak`;
- `oyun değiştirici`;
- `sınırları zorlamak`;
- `geleceği şekillendirmek`;
- `X'in gücünü açığa çıkarmak`.

They are not intrinsically forbidden. Ask whether the Turkish has a concrete proposition behind them.
