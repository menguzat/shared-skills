# Terminology system

## One concept, one preferred term

Create a terminology ledger for repeated project work.

Columns:
- concept ID;
- source term;
- preferred Turkish;
- allowed variants;
- forbidden/confusing variants;
- definition;
- audience;
- first-use treatment;
- source/authority.

## When to retain English

Retain or pair English when:
- it is the actual product/API/standard name;
- Turkish audience uses the English term as domain norm;
- translation would reduce discoverability or comprehension;
- there is no stable Turkish equivalent.

## When to prefer Turkish

Prefer Turkish when:
- an ordinary established equivalent exists;
- English is decorative rather than technical;
- audience is general;
- the term appears in UI and an English word adds friction.

## First-use pattern

Depending on audience:

`dijital ürün pasaportu (Digital Product Passport, DPP)`

or

`Digital Product Passport (DPP; dijital ürün pasaportu)`

After first use, use one approved form.

## Don't rotate synonyms

In literature, variation may be elegant.
In product and technical writing, synonym rotation can create false distinctions.

Consistency wins.
